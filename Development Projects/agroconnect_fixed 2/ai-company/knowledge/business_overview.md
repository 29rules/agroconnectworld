# AgroConnectWorld Business Overview

## Company Mission

AgroConnectWorld is a B2B agricultural marketplace platform connecting buyers and suppliers of agricultural products globally. The platform facilitates trade in commodities such as sesame seeds, spices, grains, and other agricultural products.

## Business Model

### Core Value Proposition
- **For Buyers**: Access to verified suppliers, competitive pricing, bulk order management, and streamlined procurement
- **For Suppliers**: Market access, buyer connections, order management, and business growth opportunities

### User Roles
1. **ADMIN**: Platform administrators managing users, products, orders, and system configuration
2. **BUYER**: Companies purchasing agricultural products in bulk
3. **SUPPLIER**: Companies supplying agricultural products

## Business Domains

### 1. Product Catalog
- Agricultural products (sesame, spices, grains, etc.)
- Product variations (100g, 250g, 500g, 1kg packaging)
- Product images and descriptions
- Category management
- SKU management

### 2. Supplier Management
- Supplier onboarding and verification
- Capacity management
- Certification tracking
- Supply chain transparency
- Country/region information

### 3. Order Management
- Quote requests from buyers
- Quote responses from suppliers/admin
- Order creation and tracking
- Order status management (PENDING, CONFIRMED, SHIPPED, DELIVERED)
- Order items and pricing

### 4. User Management
- User registration and authentication
- Role-based access control (ADMIN, BUYER, SUPPLIER)
- Profile management
- JWT-based authentication

### 5. Contact & Communication
- Contact form submissions
- Customer inquiries
- Support ticket management

## Business Workflows

### Buyer Workflow
1. Browse product catalog
2. Submit quote request for bulk orders
3. Receive quotes from suppliers
4. Place orders
5. Track order status
6. Receive delivery

### Supplier Workflow
1. Register and get verified
2. Add product listings
3. Receive quote requests
4. Respond with quotes
5. Fulfill orders
6. Update order status

### Admin Workflow
1. Manage users and roles
2. Manage product catalog
3. Review and approve quotes
4. Monitor orders
5. Handle contact inquiries
6. System configuration

## Market Focus

- **Geographic Scope**: Global (with focus on agricultural trade routes)
- **Product Categories**: Agricultural commodities, spices, grains, seeds
- **Transaction Types**: B2B bulk orders
- **Port Management**: Shipping and logistics integration

## Business Rules

1. **Quote Approval**: Quotes require admin approval before being sent to buyers
2. **Order Status**: Orders progress through defined statuses (PENDING → CONFIRMED → SHIPPED → DELIVERED)
3. **Role Permissions**: Different roles have different access levels
4. **Product Variations**: Products support multiple packaging sizes
5. **Supplier Verification**: Suppliers must be verified before listing products

## Success Metrics

- Number of registered buyers and suppliers
- Order volume and value
- Quote-to-order conversion rate
- User satisfaction
- Platform uptime and performance
